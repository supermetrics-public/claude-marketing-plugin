# ABM target account engagement report template

The doc for the periodic ABM program review. Audience: B2B marketing leader + sales counterparts running the program jointly.

The shape:
- Coverage and engagement summary
- Funnel by tier (if tiered)
- Most-engaged accounts not yet in pipeline (the hand-off list)
- Recommendations

### Program-level

---

## Tone notes

- The hand-off list is the most actionable part of this doc and should get sales-team attention first.
- "Reached" and "engaged" mean different things; define them consistently within the doc.
- Recommendations should split marketing actions, sales actions, and program-level changes. Each audience reads their section.
- ABM is a joint program; the doc tone should reflect joint ownership, not one team reporting to the other.
- Length: 2-4 pages.
