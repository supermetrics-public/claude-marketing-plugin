# Integrated funnel summary template

The narrative doc for both the email + paid funnel workflow and the gated-content funnel workflow. The two workflows share the same deliverable shape because both surface multi-stage conversion behavior with multiple paths.

The shape:
- Headline finding (which of the three patterns the data supports)
- Funnel flow (the visualization plus a one-paragraph read)
- Per-channel breakdown
- What this implies for budget and content strategy

## Tone notes

- The three-pattern framing in the headline is the doc's most useful insight. Pick the right one based on the data; don't hedge by listing all three.
- "Direct converter" channels aren't bad — they just don't need email nurture. The point is to not waste nurture investment on channels that don't benefit.
- For gated-content workflow specifically: the cost-per-MQL via the content vs. the user's overall cost-per-MQL is the credibility metric. Surface it clearly.
- Length: 2-3 pages. Adding more channel detail doesn't make the doc more useful.
