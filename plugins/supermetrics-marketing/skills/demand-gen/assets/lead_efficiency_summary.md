# Lead generation efficiency summary template

The one-page summary for the lead-gen efficiency comparison workflow. Structure: comparison table, the qualitative read on what's working, and the recommendation.

- **[Winner ad 1 — name or first line]** ([Platform], [CPL], [lead rate])

  - **Why it's working:** [Same treatment.]

### The "lead quality" caveat

Cost per lead alone doesn't tell us about lead quality. A few things to validate before fully committing to a budget shift:

- **Form friction.** [Platform with cheaper CPL] uses [Lead Gen Forms / a landing page]. Auto-filled forms produce more leads, but lower-intent leads. Confirm the CRM-side MQL rate before fully reallocating.
- **Audience targeting overlap.** If both platforms target similar audiences, lower CPL on one is mostly an algorithm effect. If the audiences are different (e.g. LinkedIn job titles vs. Facebook lookalikes), the comparison is genuinely about quality.
- **Sales feedback.** The clearest signal will come from sales — ask whether the leads from [winning platform] have been showing up differently in the CRM (closed-won rate, sales cycle length, deal size).

### Recommendation

[One paragraph: which platform to lean into next period, by how much, and what to monitor. Specifically: how much to shift in dollars, what efficiency improvement is expected, and what would cause us to reverse the decision.]

**Checkpoint:** Review CRM-side MQL conversion rate by source on [date — typically 3 weeks out]. If [winning platform] MQL rate is materially worse than [losing platform], partial reversal recommended.

---

## Tone notes

- The "what's working" section is what makes this deliverable useful beyond the table. Tables anyone can pull. The qualitative read on why the winners are winning is the analyst's value-add — spend the most time here.
- "Be specific about the angle" — "concrete benefits" is not a qualitative finding. "Names a specific time saving (80% faster) and pairs it with the buyer's job title (Controller)" is a qualitative finding.
- The caveat section is non-negotiable. Lead generation comparisons that skip the quality caveat get reversed within a quarter when sales complains. Pre-empt it.
- The checkpoint date matters. Converts the recommendation from a permanent choice to a measurable experiment.
