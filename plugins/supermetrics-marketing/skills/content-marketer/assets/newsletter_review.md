# Newsletter review template

The doc reviewing newsletter and email content performance. Audience: the content team, often shared with marketing leadership for the program's overall health.

The shape:
- Headline (program health)
- Content-type performance
- Subject line and timing patterns
- Unsubscribe concerns

## Tone notes

- The "what worked" hypotheses are speculation, not proof. Frame as "likely" — running A/B tests is what produces evidence.
- The unsubscribe section is where the doc earns its keep. Programs that don't audit unsubscribes lose lists without noticing.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
