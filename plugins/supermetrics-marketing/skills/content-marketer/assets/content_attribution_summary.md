# Content attribution summary template

The doc explaining which content pieces influence conversions. Audience: content team + marketing leadership.

The shape:
- Headline (which content drives the most influence)
- Role classification (discovery, middle-of-funnel, closer)
- Top performers per role
- Implications for content strategy

---

## Tone notes

- The role classification is the doc's most useful framing. Without it, content attribution becomes a list of numbers.
- "No path presence" content shouldn't be framed as failing — it might be doing important brand or SEO work. Surface it as a strategic question.
- Influence scores are relative within this doc, not absolute. Don't treat a score of 200 as objectively meaningful — it's only meaningful in comparison to other scores in the same analysis.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
