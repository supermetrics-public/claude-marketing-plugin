The doc identifying content gaps and prioritizing the next quarter's production. Audience: content team for planning; can also be shared with SEO partners.

The shape:
- Headline (size of the opportunity)
- Prioritized gap list
- Topic clusters
- Quick wins (page-2 keywords)

## What to do with the long tail

The [N+] keywords beyond the priority slate are real opportunities but small ones. Worth holding in a backlog and revisiting when:
- A pillar piece is in production and these keywords can be incorporated as sub-sections
- The team has capacity for high-volume, lower-priority production
- Competitive landscape shifts (a competitor stops covering the keyword)

---

## Tone notes

- The opportunity score is relative. Don't treat numbers as objective measures; the ranking matters more than the absolute values.
- "Quick wins" should not be more than 5-10 items. If the list grows beyond that, it competes with the new-content priority list for attention.
- Format recommendations need to match search intent — informational queries get blog posts; commercial queries get landing pages or comparison content; transactional queries get product pages.
- Length: 2-4 pages. The doc is operational, not strategic — keep it actionable.
