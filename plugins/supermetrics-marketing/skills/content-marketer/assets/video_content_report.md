## Tone notes

- Don't compare absolute view counts across platforms. A TikTok with 100k views isn't 10x better than a LinkedIn video with 10k views; the platforms have different scales.
- The composite-score ranking normalizes within each platform, but cross-platform comparisons should still be cautious. Better to talk about themes and formats than rankings.
- Production recommendations need to be specific. "Make more video content" is not a recommendation; "produce 4 behind-the-scenes shorts targeting Instagram in May" is.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
