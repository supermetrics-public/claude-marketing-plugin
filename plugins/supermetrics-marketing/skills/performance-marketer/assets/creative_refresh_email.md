# Creative refresh email template

Use this template when drafting the email at the end of the ad fatigue workflow. Replace the bracketed placeholders with actual ad names, numbers, and the user's context.

The shape of a good creative refresh email:
- Subject line that signals urgency without being alarmist
- One-line summary of what's happening
- A short table or list of specific ads (not vague descriptors — names the creative team can find)
- What you need from the creative team and by when
- An offer to walk through the data if they want

---

**Subject:** Creative refresh needed: [N] ads showing fatigue on [platform]

Hi team,

Our [platform] performance data over the last [time window] shows [N] ads have hit fatigue — frequency is up past [X] and CTR has dropped [Y]% versus the prior [window]. CPC is starting to climb on these, so we want to refresh before it eats into the [campaign/quarter] budget.

Here's what needs new creative:

| Ad name | Frequency | CTR change | Recent spend | Recommended fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Ad name 1] | [X.X] | -[Y]% | $[spend] | [New thumbnail / new copy / full refresh] |
| [Ad name 2] | [X.X] | -[Y]% | $[spend] | [Recommendation] |
| [Ad name 3] | [X.X] | -[Y]% | $[spend] | [Recommendation] |

A few notes on direction:
- The [angle/hook/format] that's been working is [description]. Worth preserving in the refresh — that's not what's broken.
- What's getting old is [the specific element]. That's where the new creative should differ.
- For [Ad name X] specifically, [any audience or placement context worth flagging].

Could we have first drafts by [date]? Happy to jump on a call to walk through the data if that's faster than back-and-forth.

Thanks,
[Sender name]

---

## Tone notes

- Keep it short. Creative teams get a lot of "please change this" emails and the long ones don't get read.
- Be specific about what's working — they need to know not to throw out the winning angle.
- Give them a deadline. "When you have time" gets deprioritized.
- The recommended fix column matters. "Full refresh" is a different ask than "swap the thumbnail" — be honest about which one this needs.
