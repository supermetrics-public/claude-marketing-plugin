# Marketing efficiency review template

The structured doc for the periodic (typically monthly or quarterly) review of actuals vs committed targets. Audience: marketing leader and their leadership/finance counterparts.

The shape:
- Headline status (on / off target overall)
- Per-metric scorecard with target, actual, variance
- Diagnosis for misses
- Action plan with checkpoint

## Tone notes

- The headline must be specific. "We're mostly on track" is not a headline — name how many metrics are on or off and where the biggest gap is.
- The trend column matters as much as the variance column. A metric 5% above target but trending down is in better shape than a metric 2% above target but trending up.
- Diagnosis is the most-skipped section in efficiency reviews and the most important. "CAC is high because acquisition is expensive" is not a diagnosis. "Facebook's CAC has risen 32% driving the blended average" is.
- Proposed actions need timelines. "We'll work on improving CAC" isn't an action; "reduce Facebook spend by $50k/week for 4 weeks" is.
- "What's going well" should be brief — 2-3 bullets max. The doc isn't a victory lap.
