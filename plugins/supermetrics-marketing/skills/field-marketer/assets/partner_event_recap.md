# Partner / co-marketing event recap template

The doc recapping a partner or co-marketing event. Often shared with the partner as a joint debrief.

The shape:
- Headline (joint outcome)
- User-only and blended views
- What worked operationally
- Renew/decline (if applicable)

## Tone notes

- This doc is often shared with the partner. Keep tone collaborative. Internal-only sections (e.g. critical assessments of the partner) belong in a separate internal doc.
- Don't claim joint outcomes without partner agreement. "Their pipeline" isn't credible until they've shared it.
- The renew/decline section is the highest-leverage content. Both sides need to come to it; preemptively naming the criteria makes the conversation cleaner.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
