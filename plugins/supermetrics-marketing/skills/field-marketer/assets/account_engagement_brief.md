# Account engagement brief template

The doc summarizing target-account engagement around an event. Audience: B2B marketing leader + sales counterparts running the ABM program.

The shape:
- Headline (account coverage outcome)
- Engagement funnel for target accounts
- Hand-off list (engaged accounts needing sales follow-up)
- Recommendations

## Accounts that didn't engage

[N] target accounts had no meaningful engagement around this event. Worth reviewing:
- Are they still the right targets, or has fit changed?
- Were they invited? (Sometimes event invitations don't reach everyone on the target list.)
- Different format needed? (Some accounts only engage with very specific event types.)

## Recommendations

### For sales
- Follow up on the hand-off list within 5 business days. Use the engagement signals (e.g. "you watched the [topic] webinar last week") as the opener.
- Update opportunity stages on accounts now showing material engagement vs. before the event.

### For marketing
- Add the [N] engaged accounts to the nurture sequence specific to this event topic.
- The [N] "warming" accounts need one more touch — propose [specific next campaign or asset].
- Update the target account list quarterly — sunset accounts that haven't engaged across multiple events.

### For the program
- Engagement rate on Tier 1 was [%] vs Tier 2 [%]. [Comment on whether the tier separation is working.]
- The event format [worked / didn't work] for [target persona]. Adjust future event design accordingly.

---

## Tone notes

- The hand-off list is the doc's most valuable content. Sales should be able to act from this table directly.
- Don't overclaim. "Engaged" doesn't mean ready to buy; it means showing meaningful signals. The bar between engaged and warming is also fuzzy.
- For accounts that don't engage, resist the urge to find a positive spin. Sometimes a target list needs refresh.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
