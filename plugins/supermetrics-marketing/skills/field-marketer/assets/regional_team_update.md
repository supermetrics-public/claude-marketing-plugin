# Regional team update template

The quick update message that goes to the regional sales team (or local partner team) about a pre-event budget shift and where registration numbers currently stand. This is closer in tone to a Slack message than to an email — even when it's sent as email, the regional team reads it on their phone between calls.

The shape of a good regional team update:
- One-line "what's happening" up top
- Current registration count vs. target
- The budget shift (what / how much / why)
- One thing you need from them (or nothing — be explicit either way)

---

**Subject:** [Event name] update — [N] regs so far, shifting budget to [channel]

Hey team,

Quick update on [event name] — we're [N] days out and at [N] registrations against a target of [N].

**What's working:** [Channel] is generating registrations at $[X] each. [Other channel] is at $[Y].

**What we're doing:** Shifting ~$[amount] from [losing channel] to [winning channel] for the final stretch. Projected to add ~[N] more registrations by event day if the pace holds.

**What I need from you:** [One specific ask, e.g. "Forward the registration link to your top accounts in [region] this week — we'd love to see [N] more from your pipeline." Or if there's nothing: "Nothing right now — just keeping you in the loop. Will send the final reg count Monday."]

[Sender name]

---

## Tone notes

- Short. If this is longer than the screen height on a phone, it's too long.
- Lead with the number. Regional teams want to know if you're hitting the target — that's the first thing they're looking for in this message.
- Name the channels by their familiar names ("LinkedIn", "Google search") rather than internal codes. Regional teams aren't deep in the marketing-ops vocabulary.
- The "what we're doing" section should sound like a decision that's already made, not a proposal. Field marketers run their own budgets; the regional team doesn't need to approve, they need to know.
- The ask should be one thing. "Forward to your top 5 accounts" works. "Help drive registrations and also share on LinkedIn and also tell your local partners" doesn't — they'll do none of it.
- If there's a Slack channel for the region, post there instead of email. The script-generated version can be pasted directly.
