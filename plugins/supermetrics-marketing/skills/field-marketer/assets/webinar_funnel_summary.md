## Tone notes

- Source-level cost-per-MQL is the single most-cited metric from this doc. Make it easy to find.
- Engagement quality matters more than registration volume for downstream conversion. Don't optimize for cheap registrations.
- Live attendance percentage trends down over time as on-demand consumption grows. Don't compare absolute numbers to webinars from 2-3 years ago.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
