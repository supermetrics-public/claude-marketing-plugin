## Recommendations

- **[Channel]**: scaling makes sense — strong new-customer acquisition at CAC within target

- **[Channel]**: balanced channels need split campaigns — separate the retargeting from the prospecting in the platform, then re-measure ROAS per segment.
- **Net new spend should flow to acquisition channels.** Returning-customer revenue grows when new-customer acquisition grows; the reverse isn't true.

## Tone notes

- The retention-channel reframe is the doc's biggest insight. Ecommerce teams over-trust channels that show high blended ROAS without breaking down the split.
- CAC numbers need a benchmark — the doc should reference the user's CAC target if it exists.
- This view sometimes conflicts with platform optimization — Facebook prioritizes ROAS, not new-customer ROAS. Flag the divergence for media buyers.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
