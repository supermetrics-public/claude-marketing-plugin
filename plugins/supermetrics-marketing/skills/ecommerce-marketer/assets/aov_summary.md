## Tone notes

- AOV averages alone hide the distribution shape. Surface the median and P75/P90 to give a fuller picture.
- Free-shipping threshold changes are operational decisions with site-wide impact. Frame recommendations as tests, not assertions.
- The just-under percentage is the most actionable number. Even 5-10% of orders just under a threshold represents real upsell opportunity.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
