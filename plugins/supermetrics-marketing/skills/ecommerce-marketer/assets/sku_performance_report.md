# SKU performance report template

The doc presenting product-level recommendations from ad-attributed analysis. Audience: ecommerce marketer + their media buyer; sometimes the merchandising/buying team.

The shape:
- Headline (the biggest SKU-level finding)
- Scale-up list
- Cut-from-ads list
- Add-to-ads (organic strength)
- Operational notes (inventory, margin)

---

## Operational watch list

- **High return rates** ([N] SKUs flagged): high ad-driven sales paired with high returns inflate apparent ROAS. Verify accurate return tracking before continuing to scale these SKUs.
- **Low inventory** ([N] SKUs flagged): currently advertised SKUs with stock that won't cover sustained ad-driven demand. Coordinate with merchandising before sending more spend.

---

## Tone notes

- Recommendations need to be actionable per SKU, not portfolio-level platitudes. "Increase spend on winners" is not a recommendation; "scale SKU001 from $3,800 to $5,500/month" is.
- Margin tiering matters more than raw ROAS. A 5x ROAS on a 15% margin product earns less per dollar than a 3x ROAS on a 60% margin product.
- Inventory coordination is non-optional. Promoting out-of-stock products burns ad spend and customer trust.
- Length: 2-3 pages.
